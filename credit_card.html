10% Cash back

As an esteemed Visa Signature Credit Cardholder, you are eligible to get 10% Cash back on dining within the first 3 months of activation of your card.



Airport Lounge Access

Relax, stretch out and enjoy a pre-flight flavour in the airport lounge at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport, Dhaka. Your Standard Chartered Visa Signature Card gives unlimited access to Airport Lounge (for the cardholder) along with up to 6 companions travelling with you.



Priority Pass

A warm welcome awaits you at over 1,000 airports around the world. The Priority Pass against your Visa Signature credit card gives unlimited free visits for you, and up to 6 (six) guest visits in a calendar year.



Double Rewards

Earn 2 Reward Points for every BDT 50 retail transaction with your Visa Signature Credit Card where other cardholders can earn only 1. The accumulated points can be redeemed for a host of privileges.



Buy One Get One Buffet

Avail Buy One Get One free buffet (breakfast, brunch, lunch, and dinner) from renowned hotels using your Standard Chartered credit card. For details click here.
